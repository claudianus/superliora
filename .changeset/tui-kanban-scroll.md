Kanban Todo board virtual scroll. The collapsed board no longer truncates lanes at five cards; it renders a viewport sized from terminal height and every card stays reachable by scrolling. Mouse wheel over the board moves the board (the transcript keeps wheel events when the pointer is elsewhere or the board is at rest), and Alt+↑/↓, Alt+PageUp/PageDown, Alt+Home/End scroll from the keyboard. A calm `↑ n more · ↓ n more` indicator shows hidden counts per direction; ctrl+t full view, card move/enter motion, and the subagents strip are unchanged.
