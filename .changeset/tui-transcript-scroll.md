A wheel-up (or PageUp/Home) expands even when the transcript does not overflow; returning to the tail collapses the previews again, and the ctrl+o pin keeps its recent-turns behavior. Tool result footers now read "⋯ N more lines — scroll to expand"; fixed-truncated surfaces (subagent subtool output, live tails) keep their plain footer.
