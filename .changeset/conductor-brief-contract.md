Conductor binds a goal-shaped contract when spawning coding workers: every task/implement JobCreate needs success_criteria. Structured fields also cover must-not-touch and verification commands; greenfield can enqueue a skeleton→fill→delete-pass chain. Goal-driver Jobs still require goal_completion_criterion. Plan Desk results expose an Implement handoff draft for JobCreate.

Use `success_criteria` on every coding Job; for 0→1 work after Plan Desk, set `delivery_mode=greenfield` and usually `greenfield_chain=true`.
